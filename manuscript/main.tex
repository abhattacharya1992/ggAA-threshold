\begin{abstract}
I study the threshold behaviour of the two-loop Type~II amplitudes for pseudoscalar pair production, $gg\to AA$, in the heavy top quark limit, in which each pseudoscalar is emitted from its own effective gluon vertex. Their analytic finite remainders are hard to evaluate near threshold because of large cancellations. I expand them in the pseudoscalar velocity $\bA$ through $\bA^6$ at fixed scattering angle. After the endpoint constants are reduced, all inverse powers of $\bA$ and the odd powers through $\bA^5$ cancel, and every coefficient is even in $\cos\theta$. At leading power the angle enters only through the logarithm of the transverse momentum $p_T$. I identify the one-loop logarithm with the double parton scattering singularity: with the overall constant fixed at threshold, the factorised formula of Gaunt and Stirling reproduces the velocity dependence of its coefficient in the forward limit to \DPSMaxDeviation{}, and a one-loop calculation of $gg\to HH$ confirms the corresponding weight for a CP-even coupling with the same constant. At two loops the equal and opposite helicity amplitudes grow as $\ln^3p_T$ and $\ln^2p_T$; the latter has no logarithm at one loop, and its two-loop logarithm is proportional to the one-loop amplitude. In the finite virtual corrections the one-loop logarithm does not interfere with the tree amplitude, and at two loops the highest logarithm, relative to the squared tree amplitude, is $-\tfrac{7}{64}\alpha_s^2\Nc^2\ln^2(2p_T/\mA)$. The released remainders are normalised differently at different orders, with a factor \FileFactor{} in the two-loop files and the one-loop files given at $\muR=1$~GeV; I convert them to a common normalisation, checked against the renormalisation group equations. For $0<\bA\leq\RecommendedBeta$ the series reproduce the complete remainders to \PtwoErrLePointTwo{} at all tested points; they are released with an evaluator.
\end{abstract}
