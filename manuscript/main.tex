\begin{abstract}
I study the two-loop Type II amplitudes for pseudoscalar pair production, $gg\to AA$, near threshold in the heavy top quark limit, where each pseudoscalar is emitted from its own effective gluon vertex. Their analytic forms contain large cancellations near threshold. I expand them in the pseudoscalar velocity $\beta_A$ through $\beta_A^6$ at fixed angle. After the endpoint constants are reduced, all inverse powers of $\beta_A$ and the odd powers through $\beta_A^5$ cancel, and every coefficient is even in $\cos\theta$. At leading power the angle enters only through the logarithm of the transverse momentum $p_T$. I identify the one-loop logarithm with the double parton scattering singularity: with the overall constant fixed at threshold, the factorised formula of Gaunt and Stirling reproduces the velocity dependence of its coefficient in the forward limit to $3\times10^{-15}$, and a one-loop $gg\to HH$ calculation confirms the CP-even weight with the same constant. At two loops, the equal-helicity amplitude grows as $\ln^3p_T$, and its logarithms follow the one-loop helicity weight in the forward limit at all velocities tested; the opposite-helicity amplitude, with no one-loop logarithm, grows as $\ln^2p_T$, and fits relate this term to the one-loop amplitude. In the finite virtual corrections, the one-loop logarithm does not interfere with the tree amplitude, and at two loops the highest logarithm, relative to the squared tree amplitude, is $-\tfrac{7}{64}\alpha_s^2N_c^2\ln^2(2p_T/m_A)$. The released remainders are normalised differently at different orders (a factor 64 at two loops, one-loop files at $\mu_R=1$ GeV); I convert them to a common normalisation, checked with the renormalisation group equations. For $0<\beta_A\leq0.2$, the series reproduce the complete remainders to $6.77\times10^{-5}$ at all tested points; they are released with an evaluator.
\end{abstract}
