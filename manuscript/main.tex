\title{\boldmath Two-loop $gg\to AA$ amplitudes near threshold and the double parton scattering singularity}

\author{Arunima Bhattacharya\,\href{https://orcid.org/0000-0003-4409-0901}{\includegraphics[scale=0.05]{orcidid.pdf}}}
\affiliation{Theory Division, IFIC, University of Valencia--CSIC,\\
E-46980 Paterna, Valencia, Spain}
\emailAdd{\PaperEmail}

\abstract{
We study the two-loop Type II finite remainders for pseudoscalar pair
production, $gg\to AA$, near threshold in the heavy top quark limit,
where each pseudoscalar is emitted from its own effective gluon vertex.
We expand them in the pseudoscalar velocity $\beta_A$ through
$\beta_A^6$ at fixed scattering angle.
After the endpoint constants are reduced, all inverse powers of $\beta_A$
and the odd powers through $\beta_A^5$ cancel, and every coefficient is
even in $\cos\theta$.
At leading power, the angular dependence enters only through the
logarithm of the transverse momentum $p_T$.
We identify the one-loop logarithm with the double parton scattering
(DPS) singularity.
With the overall constant fixed at threshold, the factorised formula
of Gaunt and Stirling reproduces its forward-limit velocity dependence
to $3\times10^{-15}$ at the tested points.
A separate one-loop $gg\to HH$ calculation confirms the CP-even weight
with the same constant.
At two loops, the equal-helicity remainder develops a cubic logarithm
of $2p_T/m_A$, whereas the opposite-helicity remainder, which has no
one-loop DPS logarithm, develops a quadratic logarithm.
Forward-limit fits support relations between these logarithms and the
one-loop helicity remainders.
At leading power, the one-loop DPS logarithm is absent from the finite
virtual interference with the tree amplitude.
At the next order, the highest logarithm in the finite virtual contribution,
normalised to the squared Type II tree amplitude, is
$-7\alpha_s^2N_c^2\ln^2(2p_T/m_A)/64$.
For $0<\beta_A\leq0.2$, the maximum relative error of the two complete
remainders at the tested points is $6.77\times10^{-5}$.
The threshold series and code for their numerical evaluation are
provided with the paper.
}
