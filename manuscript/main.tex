\title{\boldmath Two-loop $gg\to AA$ amplitudes near threshold and the double parton scattering singularity}

\author{Arunima Bhattacharya\,\href{https://orcid.org/0000-0003-4409-0901}{\includegraphics[scale=0.05]{orcidid.pdf}}}
\affiliation{Theory Division, IFIC, University of Valencia--CSIC,\\
E-46980 Paterna, Valencia, Spain}
\emailAdd{\PaperEmail}

\abstract{
The two-loop amplitudes for pseudoscalar pair production in gluon fusion, $gg\to AA$, with each pseudoscalar emitted from its own effective gluon vertex, are known in closed form, but large cancellations near threshold obscure their analytic structure. We expand the IR-finite amplitudes in the pseudoscalar velocity $\beta_A$ through $\beta_A^6$. All inverse powers and the odd powers through $\beta_A^5$ cancel, and at leading power the angular dependence enters only through $\ln(2p_T/m_A)$. We identify this logarithm with the double parton scattering (DPS) singularity: with the overall constant fixed at threshold, the factorisation formula of Gaunt and Stirling reproduces the velocity dependence of its one-loop coefficient in the forward limit to $3\times10^{-15}$, and a one-loop $gg\to HH$ calculation confirms the corresponding CP-even weight. At two loops the equal-helicity amplitude grows as $\ln^3p_T$, with the leading-logarithmic ratio of planar $\mathcal N=4$ super Yang-Mills theory, while the opposite-helicity amplitude, which has no one-loop DPS logarithm, grows as $\ln^2p_T$. In the finite virtual correction, relative to the squared tree amplitude, the highest logarithm is $-\tfrac{7}{64}\alpha_s^2N_c^2\ln^2(2p_T/m_A)$. For $\beta_A\leq0.2$ the series reproduce the unexpanded amplitudes to $6.77\times10^{-5}$ at the tested points; they provide the two-loop Type~II input to soft-gluon resummation of the pair invariant-mass distribution near $m_{AA}=2m_A$ and are released with code for their numerical evaluation.
}
