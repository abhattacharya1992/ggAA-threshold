\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

I have expanded the two-loop Type~II finite remainders for $gg\to AA$ with
two $O_G$ insertions about the pair production threshold through $\bA^6$ at
fixed scattering angle. After the endpoint constants are expressed in a
basis of \BasisCountWord{} transcendental constants, all inverse powers of
$\bA$ and the odd powers through $\bA^5$ cancel identically, and every
coefficient is even in $\cos\theta$. For $0<\bA\leq\RecommendedBeta$ the
series reproduce the two complete coefficients to \PoneErrLePointTwo{} and
\PtwoErrLePointTwo{} at the points tested, including the forward angles, in
files a factor of \SizeRatio{} smaller than those of
Ref.~\cite{Bhattacharya:2019bmd}. The remainders of that reference are
normalised differently at different orders, with a factor \FileFactor{} at
two loops and $\muR=1$~GeV at one loop; after conversion to a common
normalisation they satisfy the renormalisation group relations of
Eqs.~\eqref{eq:rg-one} and \eqref{eq:rg-two} (App.~\ref{app:normalization}).

At leading power the angular dependence enters only through the logarithm
of the transverse momentum, which I identified with the DPS singularity of
Ref.~\cite{GauntStirling2011}. The $g\to gg$ splitting weights and the
relative sign of the CP-odd subprocess amplitudes fix the momentum fraction
dependence of its coefficient; with the overall constant fixed at
threshold, this reproduces the exact one-loop coefficient in the forward
limit to \DPSMaxDeviation{}, and a one-loop $gg\to HH$ calculation confirms
the CP-even weight with the same constant. At two loops the $J_z=0$
amplitude grows as $\ln^3p_T$, with the leading logarithmic ratio of planar
$\mathcal N=4$ super Yang--Mills theory~\cite{DixonEsterlis2016} and a
$\beta_0$ term with the structure of the NLO $g\to gg$ splitting
kernel~\cite{Diehl:2019rdh}; in the forward limit both logarithms follow
the one-loop helicity weight at all velocities tested. The $|J_z|=2$
amplitude, which has no one-loop logarithm, grows as $\ln^2p_T$, and the
forward fits identify the real part of its coefficient, within the
velocities tested, as $-4\Nc$ times the one-loop forward amplitude of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:K1}. In the finite virtual corrections the one-loop
logarithm drops out because it is out of phase with the tree amplitude, and
at two loops the cubic logarithm cancels, leaving
$-\tfrac74\pi^2\Nc^2\ln^2(2p_T/\mA)$ in $V_2$, which results from the
combination of the interference of the two-loop amplitude with the tree
amplitude and the square of the one-loop amplitude.

Several questions remain open: the derivation of the overall factor
$\pi\Nc m^2$ from the factorisation formula with the effective vertices,
the mechanism that produces the $|J_z|=2$ double logarithm, and whether the
observed factor exponentiates. A general treatment of this singular region
would have to reproduce the different logarithmic powers of the two
amplitudes, their colour and flavour dependence and the reduced singularity
of the finite virtual correction; the present results provide explicit
two-loop QCD benchmarks for it, and the known two-loop $gg\to HH$
amplitudes with two effective vertices~\cite{Banerjee:2018lfq} permit the
same comparison for the scalar case. If the one-loop factorisation applies
to the full theory, the on-shell triangle form factors of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:top-dps} multiply the logarithmic coefficient by
\TopMassDPSA{} at $\mA=\TopMassMAA$~GeV; this does not determine the mass
dependence of the other terms.

The series provide the two-loop Type~II input to the hard function for the
pair invariant mass distribution near $m_{AA}=2\mA$, at NNLO for a
pseudoscalar coupled to gluons only through $G\tilde G$ and at N$^3$LO in a
2HDM (Sec.~\ref{sec:use}). The series, the evaluator, the validation
records and the code of the construction are provided with the
paper~\cite{SupplementalMaterial}.
