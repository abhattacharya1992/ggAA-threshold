\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

We have expanded the two-loop Type~II finite remainders for $gg\to AA$
with two $O_G$ insertions about the pair production threshold through
$\bA^6$ at fixed scattering angle.
After reducing the endpoint constants to a basis of \BasisCountWord{}
transcendental constants, all inverse powers of $\bA$ and odd powers
through $\bA^5$ cancel identically. Every coefficient is even in
$\cos\theta$.
At the tested points with $0<\bA\leq\RecommendedBeta$, including forward
angles, the series reproduce the two complete remainders with maximum
relative errors of \PoneErrLePointTwo{} and \PtwoErrLePointTwo{},
respectively.
The series files are a factor of \SizeRatio{} smaller than those of
Ref.~\cite{Bhattacharya:2019bmd}.
After conversion using Eq.~\eqref{eq:normalisation}, the remainders pass
the renormalisation group checks of Eqs.~\eqref{eq:rg-one} and
\eqref{eq:rg-two} (App.~\ref{app:normalization}).


At leading power the angular dependence enters only through the logarithm
of the transverse momentum, which we identified with the DPS singularity of
Ref.~\cite{GauntStirling2011}. The $g\to gg$ wavefunction factors and the
relative sign of the CP-odd subprocess amplitudes fix the momentum fraction
dependence of its coefficient; with the overall constant fixed at
threshold, this reproduces the exact one-loop coefficient in the forward
limit to \DPSMaxDeviation{}, and a one-loop $gg\to HH$ calculation confirms
the CP-even helicity sum with the same constant.
%
%
At two loops the $J_z=0$ and $|J_z|=2$ remainders develop leading logarithms $\mathcal{L}^3$ and $\mathcal{L}^2$, respectively.
The equal-helicity leading-logarithmic ratio agrees with the corresponding result in planar $\mathcal{N}=4$ super Yang--Mills theory \cite{DixonEsterlis2016}, and the comparison with the NLO $g\to gg$ splitting kernel for double parton distributions concerns its $\beta_0$ coefficient \cite{Diehl:2019rdh}.
In the forward variable $\mathcal{G}$, fits support Eq.~\eqref{eq:forward-relation} at the tested velocities in $0.05\leq\beta_A\leq0.7$.
For the opposite-helicity remainder, fits at the tested velocities in $0.05\leq\beta_A\leq0.5$ support a real double-logarithmic coefficient equal to $-4N_c$ times the one-loop forward remainder of Eq.~\eqref{eq:K1}.
At leading power, the one-loop DPS logarithm drops out of the tree interference because of their relative phase.
At the next order, the cubic logarithm of the two-loop remainder gives a quadratic logarithm in this interference.
Combining the interference with the one-loop square gives $V_2\supset-\frac{7}{4}\pi^2N_c^2\ln^2(2p_T/m_A)$.

Several questions remain open: the derivation of the overall factor
$\pi\Nc m^2$ from the factorisation formula with the effective vertices,
the mechanism that produces the $|J_z|=2$ double logarithm, and whether the
observed factor exponentiates. A general treatment of this singular region
would have to reproduce the different logarithmic powers of the two
amplitudes, their colour and flavour dependence and the reduced singularity
of the finite virtual correction; the present results provide explicit
two-loop QCD benchmarks for it, and the known two-loop $gg\to HH$
amplitudes with two effective vertices~\cite{Banerjee:2018lfq} permit the
same comparison for the scalar case.

The series provide the two-loop Type~II input to the hard function for the
pair invariant mass distribution near $m_{AA}=2\mA$, at NNLO for a
pseudoscalar coupled to gluons only through $G\tilde G$ and at N$^3$LO in a
2HDM (Sec.~\ref{sec:use}).
The series, code for their numerical evaluation, construction code and validation records are provided with the paper~\cite{SupplementalMaterial}.
