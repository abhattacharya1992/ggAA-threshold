\subsection{Leading order}

Averaging Eq.~\eqref{eq:helicity} over helicities and colours, and including a factor $\tfrac12$ for identical particles, gives the leading order partonic cross section of the Type~II amplitude,
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\dd\hat\sigma}{\dd c}
 =\frac{\bA\,C_G^4}{128\pi\hat s\,(\Nc^2-1)}\left(|\mathcal M_1^{(0)}|^2+|\mathcal M_2^{(0)}|^2\right)
 \label{eq:dsigma}
\end{equation}
for $-1<c<1$. For $\alpha_s=\BenchAlphaS$, $\cot\beta_H=\BenchCotBeta$ and $\mA=\BenchMA\,\mathrm{GeV}$ we find $\hat\sigma=\SigmaLO\,\mathrm{pb}$ at $\bA=\SigmaBeta$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:cross-section}(a)). The cross section rises as $\bA$ above threshold and as $\hat s$ at large $\hat s$, as expected for dimension-five operators, and it is of fourth order in $\as$ through $C_G^4$. In a two-Higgs-doublet model it is a small part of the total, which is dominated by Type~I (Sec.~\ref{sec:counting}).

\subsection{Finite virtual corrections}

With all remainders in one normalisation,
\begin{align}
 \sum|\mathcal A|^2&\propto\sum_i|\mathcal M_i^{(0)}|^2\left[1+\as V_1+\as^2V_2\right],
 \nonumber\\
 V_1&=\frac{2\Real\sum_i\mathcal M_i^{(0)*}\mathcal M_i^{(1),\Fin}}{\sum_i|\mathcal M_i^{(0)}|^2},
 \nonumber\\
 V_2&=\frac{\sum_i\bigl(2\Real\,\mathcal M_i^{(0)*}\Mfull{i}+|\mathcal M_i^{(1),\Fin}|^2\bigr)}{\sum_i|\mathcal M_i^{(0)}|^2}.
 \label{eq:virtual}
\end{align}
We average over the angle with the leading order weight, and evaluate $\mathcal M^{(1),\Fin}$ exactly and $\Mfull{i}$ from the series through $\bA^6$, both in the normalisation of Eq.~\eqref{eq:normalisation}. For $\alpha_s=\BenchAlphaS$ and $\muR=\mA=\BenchMA\,\mathrm{GeV}$, $\as V_1$ lies between \VoneMin{} and \VoneMax{} and $\as^2V_2$ between \VtwoMin{} and \VtwoMax{} for $\VirtualBetaMin\leq\bA\leq\VirtualBetaMax$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:cross-section}(b)). $V_1$ and $V_2$ are finite parts after the subtraction of Eq.~\eqref{eq:catani} and depend on that scheme; they are neither observables nor $K$ factors. By Eq.~\eqref{eq:normalisation}, $V_1$ depends on the scale as $\partial V_1/\partial\ln\muR^2=4\beta_0$.

Near threshold the logarithms of Sec.~\ref{sec:physics} enter the cross section in a simple way. Since $\mathcal M_1^{(0)}\to2i\mA^2$ and $|\mathcal M_2^{(0)}|\propto\bA^2$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:one-loop-threshold} gives
\begin{equation}
 V_1\;\longrightarrow\;\frac{\pi}{2}\cdot\OneLoopAone+\Imag\widehat A^{(1)}_{10}=\VoneThreshold
 \label{eq:v1-threshold}
\end{equation}
for $\muR=\mA$. The DPS logarithm drops out, because it is out of phase with the tree amplitude, and only the constant $i\pi/2$ contained in $\mathcal L$ contributes, together with the constant $\widehat A^{(1)}_{10}$. $V_1$ therefore approaches a constant, as seen in Fig.~\ref{fig:cross-section}. At two loops, write $\mathcal L=\ell+i\pi/2$ with $\ell=\ln(2p_T/\mA)$. At fixed angle the leading power terms give
\begin{equation}
 V_2=v_0+v_1\,\ell+v_2\,\ell^2,
 \label{eq:v2-form}
\end{equation}
and $\ell^3$ cancels because $\widehat A_{13}$ is real; $\widehat A_{12}$ is real as well and does not contribute to $\ell^2$. The coefficient of $\ell^2$ receives $\tfrac32\pi\widehat A_{13}=-14\pi^2\Nc^2$ from the interference of the tree and two-loop amplitudes and $\tfrac14(\OneLoopAone)^2=\tfrac{49}{4}\pi^2\Nc^2$ from the square of the one-loop amplitude, so that
\begin{equation}
 v_2=-\tfrac74\pi^2\Nc^2 .
 \label{eq:v2-log}
\end{equation}
A fit of $V_2$ at $c=\BenchCos$ and $\VtwoBetaSmall\leq\bA\leq\VtwoBetaLarge$ gives $v_2=\VtwoLogFit$, against $\VtwoLogExact$ from Eq.~\eqref{eq:v2-log}, a relative difference of \VtwoLogRelDiff. The DPS logarithms thus reach the finite virtual corrections only at two loops, where $\ln^2p_T$ is the highest power; with $\as=\BenchAs$, $\as^2v_2=\VtwoLogScaled$. At leading power $v_0$ and $v_1$ do not depend on the angle either, since the angle enters only through $\ell$; fits at $c=0$, $\tfrac13$ and $\tfrac23$ agree to \VtwoAngleSpread. Equations~\eqref{eq:leading-coefficients}--\eqref{eq:one-loop-threshold} give $v_1=\pi\widehat A_{12}+\Imag\widehat A_{11}+\tfrac72\pi\Nc\Real\widehat A^{(1)}_{10}=\tfrac72\pi\Nc\Real\widehat A^{(1)}_{10}+7\pi^2\Nc\beta_0=\VoneFit$, in agreement with the fit to \VoneFormulaDev. It does not depend on $\muR$, because $\Real\widehat A^{(1)}_{10}$ does not; the scale dependence of $V_2$ sits in $v_0$, which changes from \VzeroBench{} to \VzeroTwoMu{} when $\muR$ is doubled. Although $\ell^2$ is the highest power, it is not the largest term at these velocities: at $c=\BenchCos$ the logarithmic terms $v_1\ell+v_2\ell^2$ make up \LogShareLarge\% of $V_2$ at $\bA=\LogShareBetaLarge$ and \LogShareSmall\% at $\bA=\LogShareBetaSmall$, of which $v_2\ell^2$ contributes $-\SquareShareLarge\%$ and $-\SquareShareSmall\%$. Since $v_1$ and $v_2$ are both negative, $V_2$ has a maximum at $\ell=-v_1/(2v_2)=\VtwoPeakEll$, that is at $\bA=\VtwoPeakBeta$ for $c=\BenchCos$, and decreases above it; the angle averaged $\as^2V_2$ of Fig.~\ref{fig:cross-section}(b) falls for the same reason. The terms beyond leading power change $V_2$ by only \LeadPowerDevLarge\% at $\bA=\LogShareBetaLarge$. The coefficient $v_2$ does not depend on the conventions of the infrared subtraction: a change of the finite parts of the operators in Eq.~\eqref{eq:catani}, which depend on $\hat s$ only, shifts the remainders by lower order amplitudes multiplied by angle independent coefficients, and so changes only $v_0$ and $v_1$.

The one-loop $O_G$--$O_J$ diagrams contribute to the cross section at the same order as $V_2$, through the physical coefficient $C_J$, which depends on $m_t$~\cite{Bhattacharya:2019bmd}. By Eq.~\eqref{eq:gj-proportional} this contribution is proportional to the tree amplitude, so it shifts $V_2$ by a constant; it is not included here.

\subsection{Use in higher order calculations}
\label{sec:use}

Since $\sqrt{\hat s}/(2\mA)=(1-\bA^2)^{-1/2}$, the range $\bA\leq\RecommendedBeta$ covers the first $\ThresholdPercentPointTwo\%$ above threshold in $\sqrt{\hat s}$, that is $\sqrt{\hat s}\leq\SqrtSPointTwo\,\mathrm{GeV}$ for $\mA=\BenchMA\,\mathrm{GeV}$; $\bA=\EdgeBeta$ lies $\ThresholdPercentPointThree\%$ above threshold. We recommend the series through $\bA^6$ for $\bA\leq\RecommendedBeta$ at the angles tested, those of Table~\ref{tab:validation-grid} and the forward angles of Table~\ref{tab:forward-angles}; the logarithms diverge at $c=\pm1$, where no bound has been established. At $\bA=\EdgeBeta$ the series still reproduce both coefficients to \PtwoErrAtPointThree. The series retain the complex values of both tensor coefficients and can be combined coherently with other contributions once normalisation, Wilson coefficients and tensor basis are matched.

A threshold resummed or soft-virtual prediction of the invariant mass distribution of the pair requires, in addition to universal soft and collinear functions, the hard function of the process at each value of $m_{AA}$: the infrared subtracted virtual amplitude, weighted with the tree amplitude and averaged over the angle~\cite{Sterman:1986aj,Catani:1989ne,deFlorian:2012za}. For Higgs pairs this is the construction of Ref.~\cite{deFlorian:2015moa}, in which the two-loop virtual correction enters integrated over $t$ at fixed invariant mass. The soft and collinear functions of gluon fusion are the same for any colour singlet final state~\cite{Bhattacharya:2021nsv}. For $m_{AA}$ within a few per cent of $2\mA$ the present series give the Type~II two-loop contribution to this hard function in closed form. It enters at the order at which the remainders enter the cross section (Sec.~\ref{sec:counting}): at NNLO for a pseudoscalar that couples to gluons only through $G\tilde G$, and at N$^3$LO in a two-Higgs-doublet model. For Higgs pairs, likewise, the NNLO virtual corrections contain the diagrams with two effective vertices only at tree level and at one loop~\cite{deFlorianMazzitelli2013Virtual}, and their two-loop terms are an ingredient of N$^3$LO~\cite{Banerjee:2018lfq}.
