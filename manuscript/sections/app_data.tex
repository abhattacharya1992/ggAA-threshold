\section{Evaluation time and released data}
\label{app:data}

The series through $\bA^6$ occupy \SeriesKB~kB, against \PublishedTwoLoopMB~MB for the published two-loop files, a factor of \SizeRatio. The evaluation time was measured before the reduction of Sec.~\ref{sec:cancellations}, with the unexpanded expressions and the series in the forms used for the comparison of Sec.~\ref{sec:validation}, at $\bA=$ \TimingBetas, $c=\TimingCos$, $\mA=\muR=\TimingMA\,\mathrm{GeV}$ and $\Nc=\TimingNc$, with \TimingPrecision-digit working precision. On a first evaluation the series is \TimingFirstMin--\TimingFirstMax{} times faster. For repeated evaluations at the same point it is \TimingWarmMin--\TimingWarmMax{} times faster, with median \TimingWarmMedian; this does not correspond to a scan over new points. The two timed representations occupy \TimingSeriesMiB~MiB and \TimingExactMiB~MiB on disk, a factor of \TimingRepresentationRatio. Table~\ref{tab:timing} gives the individual measurements. \input{tables/computing_environment.tex}

The Supplemental Material~\cite{SupplementalMaterial}, which is also attached to the arXiv version of this article as ancillary files, is a self-contained package. It contains the construction files, one for each flavour coefficient and power of $\bA$ from $\bA^{-4}$ to $\bA^6$; the series through $\bA^6$ in Wolfram Language form; the reductions of the endpoint constants; the \ValidationRows{} validation records and the forward angle data; the tree-level projections; and an evaluator in Python and Wolfram Language. It also contains the Wolfram Language, C++ and Python code of the threshold construction and of the validation, the outputs of the original runs, against which the construction files and the validation records are checked, and a script that repeats the construction from the published expressions. The published expressions of Ref.~\cite{Bhattacharya:2019bmd} are downloaded and checked against checksums by a script. Each table, figure and number in this paper is generated from these data by a single command, including the check of the DPS weight in Fig.~\ref{fig:dps}, the logarithms of Sec.~\ref{sec:rate} and the normalisation checks of App.~\ref{app:normalization}. A test suite checks the evaluator against the validation records. Only the construction of the series from the unexpanded expressions requires Mathematica. The series are intended for $\bA\leq\RecommendedBeta$ at the angles tested (Sec.~\ref{sec:use}); outside this range the unexpanded remainders should be used.
