\section{Endpoint constants}
\label{app:constants}

The boundary values of the expansion contain the constants
\begin{equation}
 \mathrm{MB}[p,q,a,w]
 =\frac{a}{(p-1)!}\int_0^1\dd t\,
 \frac{[-\ln t]^{p-1}}{1-at}\,\Li_q(wt),
 \label{eq:mb-definition}
\end{equation}
with $\Li_1(z)=-\ln(1-z)$. Here $p$ and $q$ are positive integers, $p-1$
being the power of the logarithm and $q$ the weight of the polylogarithm,
so that $\mathrm{MB}[p,q,a,w]$ has weight $p+q$; the constants that occur
have $p,q\in\{1,2\}$, and $a$ and $w$ take the values $-1$, $\tfrac12$ and
$1$. Besides the elementary values $\mathrm{MB}[2,2,1,1]=\pi^4/120$ and
$\mathrm{MB}[2,1,1,1]=\zeta_3$, \ConstantCountWord{} distinct constants
occur.
They were evaluated by quadrature at high precision, independently of the amplitude data, and reduced to the basis of Eq.~\eqref{eq:constant-basis} with the PSLQ algorithm~\cite{Ferguson:1999} at \PSLQDigits{} digits. Table~\ref{tab:mb-constants} lists the results; the largest residual is \PSLQResidual, and the same relations were obtained in Mathematica.
