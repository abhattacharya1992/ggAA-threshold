\section{Flavour decomposition}
\label{app:flavour}

For $\nf=\BenchNf$ the complete coefficients are the sums
\begin{align}
 \Mfull{i}(\BenchNf)&=\Mflav{i}{0}+\BenchNf\,\Mflav{i}{1}+\BenchNf^2\,\Mflav{i}{2}
 \nonumber\\
 &\equiv a_0+a_1+a_2.
 \label{eq:nf5-sum}
\end{align}
Figure~\ref{fig:flavour-components} shows $|a_r|/|\Mfull{i}(\BenchNf)|$. The $\nf^0$ term dominates both coefficients. The $\nf^1$ term is sizeable and partly cancels it: for $\mathcal M_1$ the quantity $1-|a_0+a_1+a_2|/(|a_0|+|a_1|+|a_2|)$ lies between \PoneCancellationMin{} and \PoneCancellationMax{} over the velocities shown. At leading power the number of flavours enters with the sign opposite to the gluonic terms, through $\beta_0=(11\Nc-2\nf)/3$ in $\widehat A_{12}$, through $\Nc a_{\mathrm A}-\nf a_{\mathrm F}$ in $\widehat A_{11}$ and through $4\Nc-\nf$ in $\widehat A_{21}$ [Eqs.~\eqref{eq:leading-coefficients} and \eqref{eq:M1-L1}], which accounts for the cancellation at small $\bA$. The $\nf^2$ term of $\mathcal M_2$ is suppressed as $\bA^2(1-c^2)$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:nf2-leading-two}.
