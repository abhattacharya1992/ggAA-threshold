\section{Normalisation of the released remainders}
\label{app:normalization}

Six checks establish the normalisation in Eq.~\eqref{eq:normalisation},
using the tree amplitudes, finite remainders and Mathematica code of
the original calculation~\cite{Bhattacharya:2019bmd}.

\emph{Tree level.}
The projections of the two tree diagrams reproduce Eq.~\eqref{eq:born}
after removing a colour factor $\Nc^2-1$ present only in the $T_1$ file.
Evaluating the diagrams directly with the vertex
$iC_G\varepsilon_{\mu\alpha\rho\beta}k_1^\mu k_2^\rho\delta^{ab}$,
obtained from Eq.~\eqref{eq:EL}, gives
$|\mathcal A_{\pm\pm}|=C_G^2|\mathcal M_1^{(0)}|$ and
$|\mathcal A_{\pm\mp}|=C_G^2|\mathcal M_2^{(0)}|$
to \FeynmanDigits{} digits.

\emph{Tree and one loop.}
At threshold, the ratio of the $\nf$ coefficient in
$\mathcal M_i^{(1)}$ to $\mathcal M_i^{(0)}$ is the same for $i=1,2$.
Its imaginary part equals $-2\pi/3$ to \CataniImagDigits{} digits
at every tested point.
The one-loop diagrams proportional to $\nf$ have no cut in the
$\hat s$ channel. This imaginary part therefore comes from the
$\nf$ part of $\beta_0\ln(-\hat s/\muR^2)$ in the subtraction term
$2I^{(1)}\mathcal M^{(0)}$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:catani}.
The check fixes the tree normalisation and phase relative to the
one-loop remainders.

\emph{Scale dependence at one loop.}
At fixed $\bA$ and $c$, dimensional analysis requires
$\mathcal M_i^{(1)}/\mA^2$ to depend on $\mA$ and $\muR$ only
through their ratio.
The released remainders are instead independent of $\muR$ because
their eight $\ln\muR^2$ terms cancel.
Under $(\mA,\muR)\to(\lambda\mA,\lambda\muR)$,
$\mathcal M_{i,\mathrm{publ}}^{(1)}/\mA^2$ changes by
$-2\beta_0\ln\lambda^2\,\mathcal M_i^{(0)}/\mA^2$,
to a relative accuracy of \ScalingRawDev{}.
The original calculation renormalises the amplitude with $Z_{GG}^2$,
where $Z_{GG}=Z_{a_s}$ and $C_G\propto\as$.
Together with the scale factor
$(-\hat s/\muR^2)^{\epsilon/2}$ in the one-loop subtraction operator
of Eq.~\eqref{eq:catani}, renormalisation group invariance of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:perturbative-expansion} requires
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\partial\mathcal M^{(1)}}{\partial\ln\muR^2}
 =2\beta_0\,\mathcal M^{(0)} .
 \label{eq:rg-one}
\end{equation}
The first line of Eq.~\eqref{eq:normalisation} restores this dependence.
The corrected ratios $\mathcal M_i^{(1)}/\mA^2$ are invariant under
the common rescaling to a relative accuracy of \ScalingFixedDev{}.

\emph{Scale dependence at two loops.}
The subtraction operator $I^{(2)}$ implemented in the original code
differs from Eq.~(3.4) of Ref.~\cite{Bhattacharya:2019bmd}
by omitting the factor $(-\hat s/\muR^2)^\epsilon$ multiplying
\begin{align}
 H_g^{(2)}
 &=\tfrac12\Bigl[
 C_A^2\bigl(-\tfrac{5}{12}-\tfrac{11}{24}\zeta_2-\tfrac12\zeta_3\bigr)
 +C_A\nf\bigl(\tfrac{29}{27}+\tfrac{\zeta_2}{12}\bigr)
 \nonumber\\
 &\qquad-\tfrac12C_F\nf-\tfrac{5}{27}\nf^2\Bigr].
 \label{eq:H2}
\end{align}
With this convention, the scale dependence is
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\partial\Mfull{}}{\partial\ln\muR^2}
 =3\beta_0\,\mathcal M^{(1)}
 +\bigl(2\beta_1-8H_g^{(2)}-12C_F\nf\bigr)\mathcal M^{(0)},
 \label{eq:rg-two}
\end{equation}
where
$\beta_1=\tfrac{34}{3}C_A^2-2C_F\nf-\tfrac{10}{3}C_A\nf$.
The term $-12C_F\nf\mathcal M^{(0)}$ comes from the counterterm
of the $O_G$--$O_J$ amplitude, Eq.~\eqref{eq:gj-proportional},
in the renormalisation of Ref.~\cite{Bhattacharya:2019bmd}.

We fitted
$\partial\Mfull{}/\partial\ln\muR^2
=a\,\beta_0\mathcal M^{(1)}+b\,\mathcal M^{(0)}$
to the series at two kinematic points, for
$(\Nc,\nf,\mA)=$ \RGSets{}.
With both conversions in Eq.~\eqref{eq:normalisation}, the fit gives
$a=\RGCoefficient$ to \RGaDev{} and agreement for $b$ with
Eq.~\eqref{eq:rg-two} to \RGbDev{}, the accuracy of the series
at these velocities.
The second derivative agrees with $6\beta_0^2\mathcal M^{(0)}$,
as implied by Eqs.~\eqref{eq:rg-one} and \eqref{eq:rg-two},
to \RGSecondDev{}.
Without division by \FileFactor{}, the fit gives
$a=\RGCoefficientPublished$.
Without the one-loop scale term, $b$ differs from
Eq.~\eqref{eq:rg-two} by
$6\beta_0^2\ln(\mA^2/\mathrm{GeV}^2)$.
The agreement also fixes the reference scale of the released
one-loop remainders at $\muR=1$~GeV.

\emph{Origin of the factor \FileFactor{}.}
The original code multiplies the $O_G$--$O_G$ amplitude by
$\FileFactor=\FileFactorRoot^2$ during two-loop renormalisation,
including the tree and one-loop amplitudes used in IR  subtraction.
The one-loop step does not apply this factor.
The pole cancellation of Ref.~\cite{Bhattacharya:2019bmd} is unaffected
because all terms of the two-loop subtraction carry the same factor.
The accompanying code and data use the normalisation of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:normalisation}.

\emph{The original output.}
The Mathematica output of the original calculation confirms both
conversions.
Its one-loop remainders, normalised to the tree amplitude, have the
scale dependence of Eq.~\eqref{eq:rg-one} and agree with the first
line of Eq.~\eqref{eq:normalisation} to \ArchiveOneLoopDev{}.
The released one-loop files coincide with these outputs at
$\muR=1$~GeV.

An earlier two-loop renormalisation run, without the factor
\FileFactor{} and with the $O_J$ contribution switched off,
was evaluated at $x=1/11$, $z=3/11$.
Multiplying this result by \FileFactor{} and the tree amplitude,
then adding the $O_G$--$O_J$ counterterm, reproduces the real part
of the released $P_1$ remainder to \ArchiveTwoLoopDev{}.
The imaginary parts differ by $32C_F$ times the one-loop
$O_G$--$O_J$ amplitude, which is proportional to the tree amplitude. This run is not an alternative result.
Its renormalisation constant exceeds $Z_{GG}^2=Z_{a_s}^2$ by a
$2\beta_1\as^2/\epsilon$ pole, and the $O_G$--$O_J$ counterterm
is absent.
Its single poles therefore do not cancel with the IR subtraction
of the released version.
In the released version, cancellation of the $C_F\nf$ single pole
fixes the normalisation of this counterterm.
A June 2019 record of the original calculation requires the same
factor in both projections at \RenormPointsWord{} kinematic points.
The files and a script reproducing these comparisons are provided
with this article.
